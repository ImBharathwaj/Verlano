# Verlano – Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Overview

Verlano is a premium online clothing store that sells surplus garments from premium brands at affordable prices.

The goal is to create a **luxury shopping experience** while keeping products **accessible and affordable**.

---

# Product Vision

Create a modern fashion ecommerce platform where customers can buy **premium brand clothing at insider prices**.

Core principles:

- Luxury aesthetic
- Affordable premium fashion
- Simple and elegant shopping experience
- Fast and reliable delivery

---

# Target Users

### Primary Customers

Young professionals and students who:

- Want premium brand clothing
- Cannot afford full retail prices
- Prefer online shopping
- Value fashion and style

Age group:

18 – 35

---

# Problem Statement

Premium brands are expensive for most customers.

However:

- Factories produce surplus inventory
- Cancelled export orders exist
- Overstock garments remain unsold

Customers currently have **limited access to these products online**.

Verlano solves this by creating a **curated platform for premium surplus clothing**.

---

# Value Proposition

Customers get:

- Premium brand clothing
- At 50–70% lower prices
- Delivered directly to their home

Brand positioning:

**Luxury within reach**

---

- Shipping integration

### Nice to Have

---

# Key Features

## Product Catalog

Customers can browse products by:

- Category
- Brand
- Price

Each product page includes:

- Images
- Description
- Price
- Size options
- Stock availability

---

## Shopping Cart

Customers can:

- Add items
- Update quantity
- Remove items
- View total price

Cart persists during session.

---

## Checkout

Checkout flow:

```
Cart
 ↓
Address Selection
 ↓
Payment Method
 ↓
Order Confirmation
```

Payment methods:

- UPI
- Cards
- Net banking
- Cash on Delivery

---

# Order Management

After order placement:

1. Order created in database
2. Payment confirmed
3. Shipment created
4. Order tracking available

Customers can view order history.

---

# Shipping

Shipping is handled via Shiprocket.

Available features:

- Courier selection
- COD support
- Shipment tracking
- Delivery notifications

---

# Admin Panel

Admin capabilities:

- Add products
- Update inventory
- Manage orders
- Track shipments

Admin can view:

- sales
- orders
- inventory status

---
